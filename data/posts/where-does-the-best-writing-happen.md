*Musings on the blogging platform to rule them all*

## Where good writing takes place

- Twitter
- Notion, Google Docs
- Discord
- SMS/group chat
- Substack, blogs
- Email
- Wikipedia
- Podcasts? Interviews? AMAs?

#### Axes

- **Publicity**: Private vs few vs public
- **Length**: Chat vs shortform vs longform
- **Gamification**: view counts, reacts/likes, retweets
- **Dialogue**: Conversational (interviews, comments, chat) vs not (essays, blog posts, wikipedia)
- **Formatting**: plaintext vs chat vs headers vs images
- **Consideration**: Realtime (chat, podcast) vs Tweet vs Blog post

## Where are the best comments?

- Hacker News
- LessWrong
- EA Forum
- Manifold Discord

Misc thoughts:

- Profile: Show people’s profile? or not?

Blogs without comments: Alexey, Gwern, AppliedDivinityStudies, patio11

## Weird angles of attack

- **Highly divergent view lens:** E.g. no-image lens; everything-as-a-tweet-lens; dynamic transcription by AI
  - Also: 80k podcast examples. Title, summary, links, quotes, full transcript
  - Also: One database, many views
    - Can I throw a bunch of posts into a DB and then have LLMs figure out how to host competing views?
      - “Twitter view”
      - “LW view”
      - “Discord view”
    - View by:
      - Timeline/History
      - Best writing
- **One post, many reactions**: gather all comments & backlinks (like Lesswrong/EAF + HN + twitter reacts etc)
  - Kind of like reddit?
- **Centralizing identity**: pulling in information/publications across all of your web presence
  - Then: Some kind of LLM persona thing?
- **Notion but public**: different lens on what to read
  - Or: Anonymity? 4chan? LLM-based style hiding?
- **Ender’s Game Newsnets**: everything is public, only some people can write

- Economics of all kinds
  - Tips, bounties, payments
  - Prediction markets
  - Quadratic \*
  - Assurance contracts
  - NFTs, impact certs

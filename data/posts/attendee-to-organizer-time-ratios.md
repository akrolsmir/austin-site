#### Observations

- Parties and fun events have really high attendee:organizer time ratios
  - Maybe works because people entertain each other in a party?
  - Or because parties are good at marketing themselves?
  - Or because expectations tend to be lower for a party?
- Organizers probably prefer high ratios
  - Economies of scale: usually cheap, on the margin, to add more people
    - Amortizes \~fixed cost of planning across more folks
  - Also takeaway: extending your event time is an easy way to get higher ratios
- Attendees get more value out of low ratios?
  - More organizer time per attendee time = better experience
  - Similar to in school: students prefer individualized instruction (Bloom’s two-sigma)
  - Explains why Trading Camp should cost so much
- Conferences involve a lot of planning and upfront cost from organizer, attending one is participating in a kind of distilled 
  - Curious about how unconference-y events stack up
- Kind of want to get Mox time ratio up
  - Right now it’s in an early state. Curious how it compares to Constellation or FAR
    - Steady state, should be able to support 20ppl : 1 staff?
  - Though: maybe venue ratios are just different than event ratios

#### Questions to ponder

- How do we improve organizer efficiency or productivity?
  - Tooling, software like Airtable and Partiful?
  - Good venues like Lighthaven (and someday Mox)?
  - Putting “attendees” to work
- What would a hypothetical event that maximizes ratio be?
  - Many people (party), or very long (retreat, ski trip)
  - Or: very little organizer time (autopilot, like Taco Tuesday)

#### Caveats

- Not sure credit allocation is being done correctly in these examples. Might be double-counting or undercounting, esp on venue vs organizer
  - eg for Mox (venue host) vs QTB (organizer), or Lighthaven vs Summer Camp 
- Of course, not all attendee time is created equal!
  - Different kinds of yield from an event
    - Immediate enjoyment
    - Different kinds of relationships formed
    - Learnings and takeaways
  - Quality-adjusted attendee-hours
    - Methodology to collect this?
      - Ask people how much they would pay per hour?
      - Or observe how much they actually pay.
      - Or observe the opportunity cost of the people present (eg higher for famous speakers)
      - Ask people of ratios of time for equal “value” (1h at Manifest vs 3h at minifest)?
- And not all organizer time is created equal
  - Fulltime organizers have different opportunity costs
    - Eg technical staff might have much higher opportunity costs, but their taste may be important for a hackathon or technical event
  - Planning logistics for an event vs day-of operations are different
  - Meta events like Manifest or Summer Camp have sub-organizers
- Other costs to organize an event:
  - “relationship” cost, to send invites
  - money, to pay for food/venue/etc
  - focus or “top idea in your head” cost
  - calendar-time (not just clock time)

#### EA funders and employers should pay more

- Your employees/grantees share your values
  - So much better to give your employee \$10k, compared than an unaligned vendor \$10k
  - Giving employees more money = taps into their local knowledge
  - With political giving, it’s actually just important that individuals have enough money to donate themselves
- “Paying more” materializes better talent
  - If you want for-profit quality you should compete on for-profit axes
- Churn is very expensive
  - Onboarding costs are high, context is scarce
  - You don’t want to lose people to sth as cheap as money
- You should care about their runway (?)
  - Having good BATNAs and solid runway means that they do what’s most value-creating, rather than what pays the bills
  - Budget to explore is super important
- “What about grift?” My thesis: paying people aligns their values to yours
  - I sure feel much really warm and fuzzy towards everyone who’s ever funded me

#### EAs should negotiate their salaries

- Personal runway is important
  - Ludicrous that longtime EA employees end up worried about money and being able to pay the bills

- Your ability to donate is important
  - “Is there some group that you *would* feel strongly about?”
    - The global poor? Chickens, shrimp? Future people? AIs?
    - Your employer and your employer’s donors do not have the same values as yours
    - So think of negotiation as a values trade

- Negotiation is a muscle, a skill
  - Will need to negotiate with many other parties

- Grounding your value
  - The amount you can negotiate for, informs you how valuable your work is
    - Capitalistic heuristic of “work wherever you can get paid the most” does something valuable for the world of putting talent where it is needed
  - Salaries in EA vs salaries in tech

- Observation: EAs I know seem to not care very much about their personal salaries
  - Manifest: Rachel and Saul last year, vs Rach and David this year
  - (maybe: falls out of greater trust all around)
    - (next level argument: things like trust between professional connections is worth way more than \$10k of salary)

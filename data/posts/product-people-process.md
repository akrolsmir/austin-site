#### Product

- The thing that you make, the thing that you sell
- Pretty important! With a good product, you can make millions

#### People

- Everyone says “people are the most important”
- Kind of! Good people will fix a bad product, or set up a new one
- With the right people, you can make billions

#### Process

- Gwern: Why don’t companies replicate?
- Rule of law
  - Claim: US process is the magic that made things work
  - Not specific leaders, but the framework that was set up by the founders
- Governance
  - Needs more experiment
- With good process, “you” (the US) can make trillions
- Few companies have figured this out
- Google? with Waymo, Bets, etc
